Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-26

This policy explains what information stigready.com (“the site”) collects when you visit it or write to us, and what we do with it. The site is run by StigReady (“StigReady”, “we”, “us”), which decides how this information is used.

The short version

  • The site has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms and no checkout. You can read all of it without telling us who you are.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It sets cookies in your browser.
  • If you email us, we have your email address and whatever you write.
  • You buy StigReady images on AWS Marketplace, not on this site. AWS handles the purchase.
  • The site shows no advertising.

Google Analytics

Every page loads Google Analytics (Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-R2J9HMP1XX), a service of Google LLC. When you view a page, your browser sends Google:

  • the page address, the page title and the address of the page that linked you here (the referrer);
  • events on the page, such as page views, scrolling and clicks on links to other sites;
  • browser and device details: browser type and version, operating system, screen size and language;
  • your IP address. Google says that Google Analytics 4 uses it to work out an approximate location (country, region, city) and does not store it.

Google Analytics sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_R2J9HMP1XX, which hold a random identifier so that repeat visits can be counted. By default they last up to two years. We see aggregated reports (for example, how many people read a page, and from which countries). We do not use Google Analytics to identify you, and we send it no name, email address or other identifier of ours.

Google processes this information under its own terms. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Opting out. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use a content blocker, or install Google’s Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works the same with Analytics blocked.

Other services the site uses

  • Google Fonts. Pages load the Inter typeface from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, so your browser sends Google your IP address and browser details when it fetches the font.
  • GitHub Pages. GitHub, Inc. hosts the site. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites, such as AWS Marketplace, GitHub and X, take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Email

If you write to contact@stigready.com or support@stigready.com, for example to ask for an evidence bundle, we receive your email address, your name if your email includes it, and your message. We use them to reply and to provide what you asked for. We keep that correspondence for as long as we need it to support you and to keep business records.

Purchases on AWS Marketplace

StigReady images are sold through AWS Marketplace. You subscribe and pay there, under the terms the listing shows, and Amazon Web Services handles the purchase, billing and your account. The AWS Privacy Notice covers that processing. As the seller, we receive from AWS the information it makes available to sellers about a subscription, such as the subscribing AWS account ID and the product and usage. We use it to provide and support the product, and for our accounts.

Sharing

We do not sell the information described here. We share it only with the services named above, which process it to provide their services, or when the law requires us to.

Where information is processed

Google, GitHub and Amazon Web Services may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Your choices and requests

You can control cookies as described above. To ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it, email contact@stigready.com. We will respond as the law that applies to you requires. Information that Google, GitHub or AWS hold is managed through those companies.

Children

The site is for businesses and technical professionals. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date above.
