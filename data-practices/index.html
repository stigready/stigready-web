Data practices

Plain facts about what this website does with visitor data. Last reviewed 2026-11-03.

What the site does not do

It sets no cookies.

It runs no analytics and no advertising or tracking scripts.

It has no forms and collects no data you type in.

It stores nothing in your browser.

What still happens when you load a page

The site is served by GitHub Pages. As with any web host, your request (including your IP address and the page you asked for) reaches the host, and the host may keep access logs under its own practices.

The pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google's font servers.

Other scripts, styles and images are served from this site.

Email

If you email contact@stigready.com, we receive the message you send.
